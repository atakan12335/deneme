# English tests and English-proficiency requirements for a Turkish citizen moving to the US (as of 22 Oct 2026)

Scope note for the report writer: this is a research snapshot, compiled 22 October 2026. US immigration rules changed many times in 2025-2026 and several items below are being litigated, so always check the official source before acting. Many numbers come from search-result summaries of official pages that could not be fetched in full. Those are marked "(search summary)" and should be treated as "verify before relying".

## 1. TOEFL iBT: the January 2026 redesign, scoring, price in Turkey, Essentials, validity

### Takeaway
The redesigned TOEFL iBT launched on **21 January 2026**. It uses a new **1-6 band scale in 0.5 steps**, aligned to CEFR (4 = B2, 5 = C1, 6 = C2). Reading and Listening are now multi-stage adaptive. Core testing time is about **90 minutes** (ETS says to allow about 2 hours). Through **January 2028**, score reports also show a comparable 0-120 score. Third-party sources put the fee in Turkey at about **US$180-225** (most recent figure US$185). I could not confirm this on ETS's own site.

### Cited Findings
- **Launch date and scale.** Starting 21 January 2026, test takers get four section scores and an overall score on the 1-6 scale. Every section and the total is scored in half-point steps from 1 to 6. — [ETS score-scale update (search summary)](https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html)
- **Transition period.** Through January 2028, every score report carries both the 1-6 format and "a comparable estimate of an overall score on the 0-120 scale". — [ETS score-scale update (search summary)](https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html); [ETS blog: Understanding TOEFL iBT Score Displays in 2026](https://www.eu.ets.org/toefl/blog/understanding-toefl-ibt-score-displays.html)
- **Structure after 21 January 2026, per ETS's content page:**

  | Section | Tasks | Items | Base time |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Reading | Complete the Words; Read in Daily Life; Read an Academic Passage | 50 | 30 min |
  | Listening | Listen and Choose a Response; Conversation; Announcement; Academic Talk | 47 | 29 min |
  | Writing | Build a Sentence; Write an Email; Write for an Academic Discussion | 12 | 23 min |
  | Speaking | Listen and Repeat; Take an Interview | 11 | 8 min |

  That totals about 90 minutes, but the page says "Please allow approximately two hours to complete the test". It also says "As the test adapts, test time and items may vary." — [ETS: Test Content and Structure](https://www.in.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/about/content-structure.html)
- **Adaptivity.** ETS's April 2026 technical report says the 2026 update introduced "new task types and multi-stage testing (MST)". The overall score is "the rounded average of the section scores", rounded to the nearest half band. — [ETS research report, Papageorgiou et al., April 2026 (PDF)](https://www.ca.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/score-scale-update-2026.pdf)
- **Which sections adapt.** Third-party prep sites say only Reading and Listening are adaptive (two-stage), and Speaking and Writing are linear. — [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/toefl-2026-changes); [Study.com](https://study.com/resources/all-toefl-test-changes.html). Not confirmed on an ETS page.
- **Official conversion table, ETS report Table 6 (band to old-scale minimum):**

  | Band | Old 0-120 total |
  |---|---|
  | 6 | 114+ |
  | 5.5 | 107+ |
  | 5 | 95+ |
  | 4.5 | 86+ |
  | 4 | 72+ |
  | 3.5 | 58+ |
  | 3 | 44+ |
  | 2.5 | 34+ |
  | 2 | 24+ |
  | 1.5 | 12+ |
  | 1 | 0+ |

  Section examples: band 4 = Reading 18-21, Listening 17-19, Writing 17-20, Speaking 20-22 (0-30 scale). — [ETS research report (PDF)](https://www.ca.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/score-scale-update-2026.pdf)
- **CEFR mapping, ETS report Table 7.** The same values apply to every section and to the overall score:

  | Band | CEFR |
  |---|---|
  | 6 | C2 |
  | 5-5.5 | C1 |
  | 4-4.5 | B2 |
  | 3-3.5 | B1 |
  | 2-2.5 | A2 |
  | 1-1.5 | A1 |

  Under the old scale, the B2 floor differed by section (18 Reading, 17 Listening, 17 Writing, 20 Speaking). — [ETS research report (PDF)](https://www.ca.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/score-scale-update-2026.pdf)
- **Average scores of 2024 test takers.** Total 85.74 on the old scale (SD 18.62), or 4.40 on the band scale. Speaking was the weakest section at 3.96. — [ETS research report (PDF)](https://www.ca.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/score-scale-update-2026.pdf)
- **Origins in TOEFL Essentials.** The new Reading/Listening tasks were field-tested on more than 5,000 people "as part of the development of the TOEFL Essentials test". Turkish speakers were 153 of them (2.66%). — [ETS research report (PDF)](https://www.ca.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/score-scale-update-2026.pdf)
- **Fee in Turkey (conflicting, all third-party):**
  - US$185, citing an ETS fee list dated 28 September 2026. The same guide gives a US$69 rescheduling fee, while other sources say $60. — [LingoLeap fee guide (search summary)](https://lingoleap.ai/toefl/fees)
  - US$185 plus $40 late registration and $60 rescheduling. — [PracticeTestGeeks Turkey page](https://practicetestgeeks.com/toefl/toefl-turkey)
  - US$205-225 depending on centre and date. — [Qogent Global](https://qogentglobal.com/study-in-turkey/admissions/language-tests)
  - About US$205 as of 2025. — [Lemon Academy](https://lemonacademy.co.uk/toefl-ucretinden-tasarruf-etmenin-5-yolu)
  - US$180 for late-2025 test dates at Turkish centres. — [test-centre listing](https://lsp-dev.sp.fruitcake.dev/toefl-ibt/turkey)
- **NABP discount.** NABP's page mentions a 15% ETS TOEFL discount code ("NABPTOEFL") for pharmacy candidates. — [NABP Foreign Pharmacy](https://nabp.pharmacy/programs/foreign-pharmacy/)
- **Two-year validity in practice.** Universities apply a two-year limit. ASU says the test must be taken no more than two years before the start date, and NYU SPS will not accept TOEFL/IELTS scores older than two years. — [ASU](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/international/graduate/english-proficiency); [NYU SPS](https://www.sps.nyu.edu/join/graduate-admissions.html)

### Inferences
- For a Turkish applicant, the old benchmarks translate roughly as follows: "TOEFL 80" ≈ band 4-4.5, "TOEFL 90-100" ≈ band 4.5-5, and "TOEFL 100+" ≈ band 5+. Because band 5 starts at 95 on the old scale, a school asking for "100" may now ask for 5 or 5.5. Applicants should read each school's new-scale number, not convert it themselves.
- Speaking is the weakest section on average worldwide (mean band 3.96). Speaking minimums (e.g., nurses 4.5, pharmacists 5) are likely to be the binding constraint for many test takers.
- The test is shorter, but it adds new task types. Prep materials made before 2026 for the old format are partly obsolete.

### Gaps
- I could not load ETS's official fee page for Turkey (header-overflow and 403 errors), so no official 2026 TRY/USD fee is confirmed. The "$185" figure comes from aggregators.
- I found no reliable 2025-2026 information on whether **TOEFL Essentials** is still offered or has been discontinued. The only finding is that its item bank fed the 2026 iBT. Check ets.org.
- I could not retrieve an official list of Turkish TOEFL test centres. Third-party listings mention Istanbul, Ankara and other cities, but none was verified.
- The ETS page still says "allow approximately two hours" while the section times add up to 90 minutes. Seat time including check-in is likely about 2 hours.

## 2. IELTS Academic vs General Training: format, bands, CEFR, Turkey prices, One Skill Retake

### Takeaway
IELTS is scored in **0-9 bands**. Academic is the version US universities and health-professional screening require. General Training is mainly for migration and work in Commonwealth countries and is generally **not** accepted by US universities (UT Austin says so explicitly). The test takes about 2 h 45 min. In Turkey the computer-delivered Academic test costs about **8,500-12,210 TL** in 2026; sources conflict, so check at checkout. **One Skill Retake** is available on computer-delivered tests: one section, once, within 60 days.

### Cited Findings
- **Format.** Four sections: Listening about 30 min, Reading 60 min (including transfer time), Writing 60 min, Speaking 11-14 min. Total "2 hours and 45 minutes". The test can be taken at a centre or online in the same format. Speaking and Listening are the same in Academic and General Training. Reading and Writing differ. Academic is meant "for undergraduate or postgraduate study, or for professional registration such as doctors and nurses". — [IELTS.org: IELTS Academic](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test)
- **CEFR.** "Band scores of 8.5 and higher are recognised as C2. Band 8 is borderline." The C1 threshold "would fall between the 6.5 and 7 bands", so institutions that want high confidence of C1 may require 7. — [IELTS.org: IELTS and the CEFR](https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/compare-ielts/ielts-and-the-cefr)
- **US universities do not accept General Training.** UT Austin Graduate School: "The Institutional TOEFL (ITP) and the IELTS General Training are not accepted." — [UT Austin Graduate School (search summary)](https://graduate.utexas.edu/admissions/apply/international)
- **Same fee for both versions in Turkey.** IDP Turkey says Academic and General Training cost the same: "Academic ve General Training sınavlarının ücretleri aynıdır". For current prices it says to check the test-centre pages. — [IDP Turkey](https://ielts.idp.com/turkey/news-ielts-test-fee)
- **Turkey prices, 2026 (conflicting, all search summaries):**
  - British Council registration page (undated): Academic on computer at a test centre **TRY 8,500**; online Academic TRY 9,000; UKVI TRY 9,400. — [British Council Türkiye registration](https://www.britishcouncil.org.tr/exam/ielts/registration)
  - IELTS.org centre listing for British Council Istanbul, recently updated: Academic on computer **TRY 12,000**; UKVI Academic TRY 13,220. — [IELTS.org centre page](https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-british-side)
  - IDP Turkey: Academic **TL 12,210**; UKVI TL 13,500. — [IDP Turkey booking](https://ielts.idp.com/turkey/about/book-ielts-test)
  - A February 2026 blog says about 9,000-10,000 TL (about US$255). — [RehberPanda](https://rehberpanda.com/blog/2026-e-tep-maliyet-karsilastirmasi-toefl-ielts-detay/)
- **International price range.** US$245-310 depending on country. — [ielts.international registration guide](https://www.ielts.international/tr/ielts-registration)
- **One Skill Retake rules.** Offered for computer-delivered IELTS Academic, General Training and UKVI. A candidate can retake one skill, once per original test, within 60 days. "IELTS for Academic and General Training on computer One Skill Retake is currently not available for tests taken in the USA". — [British Council: One Skill Retake](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/one-skill-retake)
- **IDP Turkey on One Skill Retake.** IDP Turkey publishes an explainer comparing One Skill Retake with a remark (Enquiry on Results). — [IDP Turkey article](https://ielts.idp.com/turkey/about/news-and-articles/article-ielts-enquiry-on-result-vs-one-skill-retake)
- **US acceptance of One Skill Retake.** Whether a US school accepts One Skill Retake results is up to the institution. — [IELTS.org update on One Skill Retake](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/update-on-ielts-one-skill-retake)

### Inferences
- A Turkish applicant aiming at the US should book **IELTS Academic**, not General Training or UKVI. UKVI costs more and is only needed for UK visas.
- One Skill Retake can be done in Turkey but not in the US, so it is a reason to test in Turkey before moving. Applicants should confirm that their target US school accepts it.
- The lira price has moved quickly (6,000 TL on older pages, 8,500-12,210 TL now). Advise readers to check the live booking page.

### Gaps
- I found no official current One Skill Retake fee in TL.
- IELTS.org's chart mapping bands to **B2/B1** was an image I couldn't read. Bands 5.5-6.5 are commonly cited as B2 but this was not confirmed in text.
- The official IELTS validity statement was not fetched. US schools apply a two-year limit (see section 5).
- The paper-based test is still offered in Turkey per IDP's booking page title, but the paper price was not confirmed.

## 3. Duolingo English Test (DET): price, format, scale, US acceptance

### Takeaway
The DET is an online, adaptive test taken at home. It lasts about 1 hour, is scored **10-160**, returns results in about 2 days, and scores are valid 2 years. It costs about **US$70** per attempt as of 2026, far cheaper than TOEFL or IELTS. Most large US universities accept it for admission, typically at **105-135+**. Some flagships don't (e.g., Univ. of Michigan undergrad), and it is **not** accepted for health-care VisaScreen, pharmacist FPGEC or teaching-assistant screening.

### Cited Findings
- **Price, scale, length, validity.** US$70 (2026); 10-160 scale, with scores above 120 indicating proficiency; about 1 hour; adaptive; valid 2 years. — [Wikipedia: Duolingo English Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duolingo_English_Test)
- **Price history and discounts.** The current price is said to date from February 2025, and older figures ($49, $59, $65) still circulate online. A $60 voucher is reportedly available through a partner. — [Cialfo guide](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/?p=1503); [DuolingoGuides](https://duolingoguides.com/?p=7310)
- **Stale prices on official partner pages.** Some go.duolingo.com partner pages still show $49. — [go.duolingo.com/mccallie](https://go.duolingo.com/mccallie)
- **Scoring.** The overall score is the average of the Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking subscores, rounded up to the next 0 or 5. — [Mastersportal](https://mastersportal.com/articles/3551/how-is-duolingo-english-test-scored.html)
- **Timing and results.** About 1 hour of uninterrupted time: an adaptive section of about 45 min plus writing and speaking samples of about 10 min. Scores in about 2 days. — [DET official guide (PDF)](https://englishtest-static.duolingo.com/media/resources/test-taker-guide/DET%20Official%20Guide%202024%20English.pdf); [englishtest.duolingo.com](https://englishtest.duolingo.com/)
- **Acceptance.** More than 6,500 programs worldwide, including all eight Ivy League universities, and more than 4,300 in North America. Some applicants may still need other proof of English. — [DET accepting institutions](https://englishtest.duolingo.com/test_takers/accepting_institutions); [DET blog](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/duolingo-english-test-fastest-growing-study-abroad/)
- **2026 limits on acceptance:**
  - University of Michigan "does not accept results from Duolingo exams". — [UMich SMTD international admissions (search summary)](https://smtd.umich.edu/admissions/international-admissions/)
  - UIUC: DET cannot satisfy the teaching-assistant English requirement. — [UIUC Graduate College](https://grad.illinois.edu/admissions/international-applicants)
- **Not on the HHS/TruMerit list for health-care workers.** The approved tests are Cambridge B2/C1/C2, TOEFL iBT, IELTS Academic, MET, OET and PTE Academic. — [TruMerit VisaScreen](https://www.trumerit.org/services/certification/visascreen-visa-credentials-assessment/)

### Inferences
- For a Turkish student whose only goal is a mainstream US university, the DET is the cheapest option (about $70 versus about $185 for TOEFL or 8,500-12,000 TL for IELTS) and can be taken from home in Turkey. Check every target school first, because some do not accept it.
- Anyone heading for a licensed profession (nurse, pharmacist, physician) or a TA-funded PhD needs TOEFL, IELTS, OET or PTE instead.

### Gaps
- No official Duolingo page fetched in this session states the $70 price directly. Wikipedia and several guides agree on it.
- I found no evidence of a DET score-scale change in 2025-2026.

## 4. Other tests: PTE Academic, Cambridge C1 Advanced, OET, MET

### Takeaway
PTE Academic is offered in Turkey (one source says about ₺10,000; a Pearson-linked site gives a US$140-310 range) and is accepted by many US schools and by HHS for health workers. Cambridge C1 Advanced is on the HHS list, but this research did not confirm how widely US universities accept it. OET is mandatory for foreign-trained physicians (ECFMG) and accepted for nurses.

### Cited Findings
- **PTE price in Turkey.** ₺10,000 for PTE Academic (also UKVI/Core), last updated 2 June 2026, according to a commercial prep site. — [AlfaPTE](https://alfapte.com/read-post/pte-exam-centers-fees-turkey)
- **PTE price range and centres.** A Pearson-linked booking page lists centres in Istanbul, Ankara, Adana and other cities, gives US$140-310, and says centres set their own fees. — [PTE Turkey centres](https://etp-dev.fruitcake.dev/pte/turkey)
- **US university acceptance of PTE.** ASU accepts IELTS, PTE, TOEFL or DET taken within two years of the start date. — [ASU graduate English proficiency](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/international/graduate/english-proficiency)
- **HHS/TruMerit-approved tests.** Cambridge (B2 First, C1 Advanced, C2 Proficiency), TOEFL iBT, Academic IELTS, MET, OET and Pearson PTE Academic. TOEIC is not listed. — [TruMerit VisaScreen](https://www.trumerit.org/services/certification/visascreen-visa-credentials-assessment/)
- **OET for physicians.** OET Medicine is required for ECFMG certification (see section 7). — [ECFMG Pathways FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html)

### Inferences
- PTE is fully computer-scored and results come quickly, which can suit people who struggle with face-to-face speaking. Its HHS speaking minimum for nurses is now very high (76; see section 7).

### Gaps
- I found no source on how widely **Cambridge C1 Advanced** is accepted by US universities. It is far more common in UK and EU admissions, so check each school.
- No official Pearson TRY price was found.
- OET and MET prices in Turkey were not researched.

## 5. Typical score requirements: US undergraduate, graduate, community college, conditional or ESL pathways, and CEFR equivalents

### Takeaway
On the new TOEFL scale, mainstream US universities in 2026 ask for about **4-4.5** for undergraduate entry (IELTS 6.5, DET 110-120). Selective schools and many graduate programs ask for **5-5.5** (IELTS 7-7.5, DET 125-135). Community colleges accept much lower scores (e.g., Santa Monica College: IELTS 5.0, DET 75) and route weaker students into ESL or intensive English. Admission with a language condition is common at community colleges and through partner language schools. An I-20 cannot be issued on conditional admission alone; an ESL program has to be the actual program of study.

## 6. Where English is formally or practically required in US immigration pathways

### Takeaway
Only **naturalization** (citizenship) has a formal federal English test. It covers reading, writing and speaking, plus a civics test. For filings from **20 Oct 2025**, the civics part uses the 2025 version (20 questions from 128, need 12 correct). Student and exchange routes require English **through the school or sponsor**: the I-20 confirms the student met the English requirement, and J-1 sponsors must objectively measure English under 22 CFR 62.10. Work visas, employment-based and family green cards, and the Diversity Visa have **no English test**, though the job market effectively requires English. A Turkey-born person was eligible for DV-2026. DV-2027 registration was ordered by a court to open **1 Nov 2026**, but DV *issuance* is currently paused worldwide.

### Cited Findings
**F-1 student**
- The DSO may issue an I-20 only after the student meets the school's English requirement, and not on conditional admission. — [SEVP S13.2 (PDF)](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/assets/sevp-PGS132-i20.pdf)
- University visa guidance says a student "must be proficient in English unless you are coming to the United States to participate in an English language training program", and the school may make special arrangements to teach English. — [Minnesota State Mankato F-1 requirements](https://services.mnsu.edu/TDClient/43/Portal/KB/PrintArticle?ID=827)

**J-1 exchange visitor**
- Sponsors must screen applicants for English sufficient for the program and for everyday life, "determined by an objective measurement of English language proficiency". — [22 CFR 62.10, Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/62.10)
- Accepted methods are a recognized English test, signed documentation from an academic institution or English language school, or a documented interview by the sponsor (in person or by video, or by phone if video isn't viable). — [22 CFR 62.10](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/62.10); [Johns Hopkins OIS](https://ois.jhu.edu/Department_Administrators/Sponsoring_J1_Exchange_Visitors/J1_Scholar/English_Proficiency/index.html)
- NC State, for example, accepts a recognized test no more than 2 years old, or an interview rated at CEFR B1 or above. — [NC State](https://internationalservices.ncsu.edu/english-language-proficiency-for-j-1-scholars/)

**H-1B (2025-2026 changes, no English test)**
- **$100,000 fee.** A 19 September 2025 proclamation imposes a $100,000 payment for new petitions for beneficiaries outside the US without a valid H-1B visa. Change-of-status and extension filings are excluded.
- **Legal status of the fee is unclear.** A D.C. district court upheld it on 23 December 2025. Another report says a Boston judge vacated it. The proclamation was set to expire on 21 September 2026; whether it was extended is unknown.
- — [Fox Rothschild](https://www.foxrothschild.com/publications/major-changes-to-the-h-1b-visa-application-process-for-fiscal-year-2027); [Gunster](https://www.gunster.com/newsroom/publications/dhs-issues-final-rule-to-replace-random-selection-h-1b-cap-lottery-with-weighted-selection-process-based-on-wage-levels-for-h-1b-cap-allocation); [AILA FY2027 cap page](https://www.aila.org/library/featured-issue-fy2027-h-1b-cap-season?page=2)
- **Wage-weighted lottery.** A DHS final rule replaced the random lottery with wage-level weighting (Levels I-IV), effective about 27 February 2026 for the FY2027 cap season. Registration opened 4 March 2026. — [Gunster](https://www.gunster.com/newsroom/publications/dhs-issues-final-rule-to-replace-random-selection-h-1b-cap-lottery-with-weighted-selection-process-based-on-wage-levels-for-h-1b-cap-allocation); [Buchanan Ingersoll](https://www.bipc.com/fy-2027-h-1b-cap-registration-opens-march-4,-2026-key-changes-and-action-items)

**Diversity Visa**
- **Who is eligible.** Eligibility depends on country of birth. Countries that sent more than 50,000 immigrants in the prior five years are excluded. The DV-2026 ineligible list had 19 countries (Bangladesh, Brazil, Canada, China incl. Hong Kong, Colombia, Cuba, Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Haiti, Honduras, India, Jamaica, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Philippines, South Korea, Venezuela, Vietnam). **Turkey is not on it.** — [Buchanan Ingersoll DV-2026 overview](https://www.bipc.com/diversity-visa-program-overview-for-fiscal-year-2026); [DV-2026 Plain Language Instructions (State Dept PDF)](https://travel.state.gov/content/dam/visas/Diversity-Visa/DV-Instructions-Translations/DV-2026-Instructions-Translations/DV%202026%20Plain%20Language%20Instructions%20and%20FAQs.pdf)
- **Education or work requirement.** Either a high school education (a completed 12-year course of formal elementary and secondary education; GED and correspondence programs not accepted) or 2 years of work experience within the past 5 years in an occupation needing at least 2 years of training or experience (judged against O*NET). No English test is listed. — [Buchanan Ingersoll](https://www.bipc.com/diversity-visa-program-overview-for-fiscal-year-2026)
- **Embassy warning.** The US Embassy in Türkiye warns that claiming the wrong chargeability country (e.g., choosing Turkey when born in Iran) can cause disqualification. — [US Embassy Türkiye DV page (search summary)](https://tr.usembassy.gov/diversity-immigrant-visa-dv/)
- **DV-2027 registration status:**
  - Registration was delayed and the program was paused in December 2025.
  - On 14 October 2026 the S.D. California issued a preliminary injunction ordering the State Department to restart it. The timetable: dates announced by 29 Oct 2026, registration opens 1 Nov 2026 for 30 days, selectees notified 3 Dec 2026, processing until 30 Sep 2027.
  - Applicants must give valid passport information, and there is a **$1 registration fee**.
  - The government may appeal or seek a stay.
  - — [Visa Lawyer Blog, 18-19 Oct 2026](https://www.visalawyerblog.com/federal-judge-orders-reopening-of-2027-diversity-visa-lottery-registration-by-november-1st/); [TXLRS](https://txlrs.org/blog-post/texas-dv-2027-diversity-visa-registration-court-order-october-2026); one source names the judge as Jinsook Ohta.
- **DV issuance pause history:**
  - A December 2025 DV issuance pause ceased on 28 August 2026 after *Medani v. Trump*.
  - On **31 August 2026** new State Department guidance paused DV issuance again for **all nationalities**. Applicants can still interview, but no DV visas are being issued.
  - DV-2026 eligibility ended 30 September 2026.
  - — [Clinch Law, 10 Sep 2026](https://blog.clinchlaw.com/us-immigration-law-and-insights/is-the-green-card-paused-the-75-country-policies-court-rulings-and-turkey/)

**Naturalization (citizenship)**
- **English test.** Reading: read aloud 1 of 3 sentences correctly. Writing: write 1 of 3 sentences correctly. Speaking and understanding: assessed by the officer during the N-400 interview. — [USCIS: Study for the Test](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/study-for-the-test)
- **Civics test, by filing date:**
  - N-400 filed **before 20 Oct 2025**: 2008 version, up to 10 questions from 100, 6 correct to pass.
  - Filed **on or after 20 Oct 2025**: 2025 version, 20 questions from 128, 12 correct to pass. "Officers will stop asking questions when an alien answers 12 questions correctly, or 9 questions incorrectly."
  - — [USCIS: Study for the Test](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/study-for-the-test); [USCIS 128 questions PDF](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/questions-and-answers/2025-Civics-Test-128-Questions-and-Answers.pdf)
- **65/20 rule.** Applicants aged 65+ with 20+ years as a permanent resident get 10 questions from a special bank of 20 (marked with an asterisk) and may test in the language of their choice. — [USCIS: Study for the Test](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/study-for-the-test)
- **50/20 and 55/15 rules.** Applicants aged 50+ with 20 years as an LPR, or 55+ with 15 years, are exempt from the English requirement. They still take civics, in their chosen language through an interpreter. The USCIS Policy Manual's summary table also marks 65/20 as English-exempt. — [USCIS Policy Manual Vol. 12, Part E, Ch. 2](https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-e-chapter-2)
- **Disability exception (Form N-648) tightened.** For applications filed on or after 13 June 2025, the medical certifier must explain how the condition prevents the applicant from meeting the requirement. A diagnosis alone is not enough, and multiple submissions may raise credibility concerns. — [EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/uscis-updates-disability-exception-policy-for-naturalization-effective-june-13-2025/); [ILRC](https://www.ilrc.org/sites/default/files/2025-08/Naturalization-Update-Policy-Manual-Revisions-to-the-Disability-Waiver.pdf)
- **More changes may follow.** USCIS called the 2025 civics test the first step of a multi-phase plan, alongside more intensive screening. — [ImmigrationQuestion](https://immigrationquestion.com/blog/news/uscis-naturalization-test-2025-changes/)

### Inferences
- **EB-2, EB-3 and family green cards:** none of the official materials reviewed mention an English test. The first federal English test a green-card holder faces is at naturalization, after normally 5 years, or 3 if married to a US citizen. In practice, though, EB-2/EB-3 employers need English-capable workers, and the consular interview is in English or through an interpreter.
- **H-1B:** no language test exists, but the employer and the specialty occupation effectively require professional English. The new wage-weighted lottery favours higher-paid roles, which usually need strong English.
- **F-1:** the consular officer at the US Embassy in Ankara or the Consulate in Istanbul sees the I-20 and may judge whether the applicant can follow the course. Weak spoken English at the interview is a practical risk even with a valid score.
- **DV:** the lottery needs no English, but selectees must interview at the embassy, and they face the same job-market reality as anyone else.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch 9 FAM 402.5 to quote how consular officers assess F-1 English.
- No DV-2027 or DV-2028 eligible-country list had been published as of 22 Oct 2026. The State Department is due to announce requirements by 29 Oct 2026 under the court order, so Turkey's DV-2027 eligibility is unconfirmed, though likely since Turkey was eligible in prior cycles.
- No official text was fetched for EB-2/EB-3 or family pathways confirming the absence of a language test.
- The current status of the H-1B $100k fee (after its 21 Sep 2026 expiry date) and the outcome of the Boston ruling are unknown.

## 7. Professional licensing and job-specific English requirements

### Takeaway
Health-care workers entering on work visas or green cards need a **VisaScreen certificate** from TruMerit (formerly CGFNS) with HHS-approved test scores. **New, mostly higher minimums apply to tests taken after 12 May 2026**, and TOEIC is no longer accepted for registered nurses. Physicians need **OET Medicine** for ECFMG certification. Pharmacists need a single-sitting **TOEFL iBT** (new-scale minimums since 21 Jan 2026), with OET accepted from 11 Jan 2027. Since **25 June 2025**, truck drivers who fail a roadside English check are placed **out of service**, with the policy revised 16 April 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Health-care workers (VisaScreen / HHS)**
- **May 2026 update.** On 12 May 2026, HHS approved new English tests and scores for health-care worker certification. HHS has sole authority, in consultation with the Department of Education. — [USCIS: Health Care Worker Certification (search summary)](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/health-care-worker-certification)
- **TruMerit notice.** "USCIS and HRSA have updated their requirements for ELP testing". The new scores apply to exams taken after 12 May 2026; earlier exams are assessed under the old table. VisaScreen certificates have been issued digitally since 1 Nov 2025. Applicants educated in English in the UK, Australia, Canada (except Quebec), NZ, Ireland or the US are exempt. CGFNS has been renamed TruMerit. — [TruMerit VisaScreen](https://www.trumerit.org/services/certification/visascreen-visa-credentials-assessment/)
- **Registered-nurse minimums after 12 May 2026** (a recruiter's summary, not the official table):
  - TOEFL iBT: overall 4.5, Speaking 4.5, Reading/Listening/Writing 3.5
  - IELTS: 6.5 overall, 7.0 Speaking (unchanged)
  - OET: Speaking 360 (was 350), Writing 350 (was 300), Reading 340 (was 300), Listening 320 (was 300)
  - PTE Academic: overall 55, Speaking 76 (was 50; TruMerit says 63 since 1 Dec 2024), others 62 (was 50)
  - MET: overall 58 (was 55), Speaking 59 (was 55)
  - TOEIC: no longer accepted for RNs and other bachelor-level health workers
  - — [HealthStaff blog](https://candidate.healthstaff.org/blog/updated-english-testing-requirements-for-international-nurses-coming-to-the-u-s)
- **Other reporting on the change.** Requirements "have increased in most cases". The table no longer separates IELTS Academic from General Training. Occupational therapists can now use more tests. — [TOEFL Resources](https://www.toeflresources.com/blog/new-hrsa-english-test-score-requirements/)
- **Earlier proposal.** In Nov 2024, HHS proposed moving OET from letter grades to numeric scores, with 300 for most skills and 350 for Speaking. — [Federal Register 2024-25854](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/11/07/2024-25854/standardized-tests-and-minimum-passing-scores-for-foreign-health-care-workers-to-demonstrate-english)

**Physicians (ECFMG)**
- OET Medicine is required for all 2026 Pathways applicants "regardless of citizenship or native language", with no exceptions. The score must be from on or after 1 Jan 2024 for 2026 Pathways, and on or after 1 Jan 2025 for 2027 Pathways. — [ECFMG news, 30 Jul 2025](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/2025/07/30/important-information-on-2026-pathways-application-to-open-in-august); [ECFMG Pathways FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html)
- Minimums are **350** on Listening, Reading and Speaking and **300** on Writing, in one administration (for tests from 1 April 2022). A failed sub-test means retaking all of them. — [ECFMG OET page (search summary)](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/oet.html); [ECFMG 2022 announcement](https://ecfmg.org/news/2022/03/31/detailed-information-available-on-2023-pathways-updated-passing-requirement-for-oet-medicine)

**Pharmacists (NABP FPGEC)**
- TOEFL iBT section minimums for tests on or after 21 Jan 2026: **Reading 4, Listening 5, Writing 4.5, Speaking 5**. For earlier tests the bulletin's old-scale minimums were reported as 21/22/22/25, which conflicts with older commonly cited figures; verify. All four sections must be passed in one sitting, and at-home TOEFL is not accepted. — [NABP FPGEC Bulletin (search summary)](https://read.nxtbook.com/nabp/bulletin/fpgec_bulletin/step_3_toefl.html)
- From **1 Nov 2026**, MyBest scores can be submitted for the 2027 FPGEE. OET is accepted for exams taken from **11 Jan 2027**. — [NABP Foreign Pharmacy](https://nabp.pharmacy/programs/foreign-pharmacy/)

**Truck drivers (FMCSA)**
- **Policy change.** FMCSA issued an enforcement policy on 20 May 2025 reversing 2016 guidance. Inspectors began placing drivers out of service for English-language-proficiency violations on 25 June 2025. The underlying rule, 49 CFR 391.11(b)(2), did not change. — [DISA](https://disa.com/news/fmcsa-english-language-proficiency-rule-guide/); [Trucking Dive](https://www.truckingdive.com/news/fmcsa-reveals-english-language-proficiency-guidelines/748554/)
- **The roadside test.** A short English interview with no interpreters or translation apps, then highway-sign recognition. Failing either can lead to an immediate out-of-service order. — [TT News](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/english-proficiency-rule-2026)
- **Scale.** Early data showed 1,212 violations. An ATA executive later estimated about 2,000 drivers sidelined per month. — [The Truckers Report](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/news/1200-truck-drivers-taken-off-u-s-roads-failing-english-language-tests); [CCJ](https://www.ccjdigital.com/regulations/safety-compliance/article/15833851/how-fmcsa-english-proficiency-impacts-trucking-capacity)
- **April 2026 revision.** The policy was revised on 16 April 2026, refining the sign assessment and clarifying US-Mexico border commercial zones. — [TT News](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/english-proficiency-rule-2026)

### Inferences
- Turkish nurses should plan for at least IELTS Academic 6.5 with Speaking 7.0, or TOEFL 4.5 with Speaking 4.5, i.e., solid B2 to C1. The PTE route got much harder (Speaking 76).
- Turkish physicians face a C1-level OET requirement before ECFMG certification.
- CDL and trucking, a common immigrant job, now has a practical spoken-English floor that is enforced at the roadside.

## 8. 2025-2026 US policy changes affecting Turkish citizens' visa process

### Takeaway
Turkey was **not** on the June 2025 / December 2025 travel-ban lists (Proclamation 10998, 39 countries) or on the January 2026 75-country immigrant-visa pause, which ended 21 Aug 2026. Turkish applicants are still affected by **worldwide** measures:
- the end of most interview waivers (2 Sep 2025)
- interviews only in the country of nationality or residence (6 Sep 2025)
- public social-media vetting for F/M/J applicants (18 Jun 2025)
- the worldwide DV issuance pause (31 Aug 2026)
- the H-1B $100k fee and weighted lottery
- a legislated but apparently not-yet-collected **$250 visa integrity fee**

B1/B2 wait times in Ankara fell sharply after 2024's 1.5-2 year backlogs. Executive Order 14224 (1 Mar 2025) designated English the official language but changes no visa rule.

### Cited Findings
- **Turkey and the country lists.** Turkey is not among the 39 countries in Proclamation 10998 and not on the 75-country immigrant-visa pause list. That pause began 21 Jan 2026 and ceased 21 Aug 2026 after *CLINIC v. Rubio*. USCIS adjudication holds for the 39 countries were vacated by D.R.I. on 5 Jun 2026. — [Clinch Law, 10 Sep 2026](https://blog.clinchlaw.com/us-immigration-law-and-insights/is-the-green-card-paused-the-75-country-policies-court-rulings-and-turkey/); [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2806/2026-01-29-state-department-suspends-immigrant-visa-processing-75); [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/news/federal-court-order-ends-75-country-immigrant-visa-pause-state-dept-confirms/)

Applicants were told to set social-media profiles to public, and limited access may be treated as failure to provide information. Posts may schedule fewer F/M/J cases. — [Yale OISS](https://oiss.yale.edu/news/department-of-state-announces-enhanced-social-media-vetting-and-resumption-of-visa-interview-scheduling); [Gunster](https://www.gunster.com/newsroom/publications/recent-updates-on-department-of-states-dos-social-media-vetting-of-student-and-exchange-visitor-applicants)

  - After a State Department update, Ankara's next B1/B2 appointment was shown as about **15 days**, down from 5 months. — [Capital](https://www.capital.com.tr/haberler/tum-haberler/abd-vize-bekleme-surelerini-guncelledi-ankara-buyukelcisinden-mesaj-var)
  - In 2024 the waits had been 738 days (Istanbul) and 596 days (Ankara). — [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/abd-vizesi-icin-bekleme-suresi-2-yili-asti-3694204)
  - A recent post exists on E-2/H-1B/L-1/O-1 stamping in Ankara and Istanbul (15 Oct 2026). — [Clinch Law](https://blog.clinchlaw.com/us-immigration-law-and-insights/is-the-green-card-paused-the-75-country-policies-court-rulings-and-turkey/)

- Turkish F-1/J-1 applicants should expect an in-person interview in Ankara or Istanbul, with no option to apply in a third country, and online-presence screening. They should apply early.

## 9. How English level affects earnings and life in the US

### Takeaway
English proficiency is consistently linked to large earnings gains for immigrants:
- about **17%** in the classic Chiswick & Miller estimate (1989 data)
- **17-33%** across the literature
- English-proficient workers' median earnings about **39% higher** than those of limited-English workers (2012 ACS)
- a **72.5%** causal earnings effect in a 2026 Boston Fed study of New England

Part of the raw gap reflects selection. Nearly half of US immigrants (47.1% in 2024) are classified as limited English proficient.

### Cited Findings
- **Chiswick & Miller (1995).** The earnings gain from English proficiency for adult male immigrants (1989) was about **17%**, roughly equal to 3 years of schooling. The literature range is 17-33% (Chiswick & Miller 1995; Bleakley & Chin 2004). — [IZA DP 2974 (PDF)](https://docs.iza.org/dp2974.pdf); [Univ. of Akron paper (2010)](https://fye.uakron.edu/sociology/faculty-staff/The%20Conditional%20Relationship%20between%20English%20Language%20Proficiency%20and%20Earnings%20among%20US%20Immigrants%202010.pdf)
- **Caveats.** Part of the premium likely reflects unobserved factors (self-selection, job-shopping, legal status). Historical 1900-1930 estimates are smaller (5-9%). — [Univ. of Akron paper](https://fye.uakron.edu/sociology/faculty-staff/The%20Conditional%20Relationship%20between%20English%20Language%20Proficiency%20and%20Earnings%20among%20US%20Immigrants%202010.pdf); [UQ Economics](https://economics.uq.edu.au/node/1379)
- **Brookings (2014, 2012 ACS).** English-proficient workers' median earnings are 39% higher than LEP workers' overall, and 30% higher among bachelor's holders. LEP workers earn 25-40% less on average, with a gap of 17%-135% depending on the metro area. — [Brookings: Six questions about the LEP workforce](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/six-questions-about-the-limited-english-proficient-lep-workforce/); [Brookings press release (PDF)](https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/metro_20140924_investing_in_english_skils_press_release.pdf)
- **Boston Fed, Osborne Jackson, "English Proficiency and Labor Market Outcomes in New England", Research Report 26-2, April 2026:**
  - Uses ACS data on the foreign-born, 2000-2023.
  - Greater English proficiency raises immigrants' average annual earnings by **72.5%** and labor-force participation by about **12.9%**, with no detectable effect on employment among those in the labor force.
  - The earnings effect ranged from 61.3% to 112.5% across sub-periods.
  - The effect grows with education and shrinks with age.
  - Nationally, 55.6% of immigrants speak English "very well", versus 60.7% in New England.
  - — [Boston Fed NEPPC RR 26-2 (PDF)](https://www.bostonfed.org/-/media/Documents/Workingpapers/PDF/2026/neppcrr2602.pdf)
- **MPI (2015 PIAAC analysis).** Found a "severe wage penalty imposed by low education and low English skills". Immigrants with low literacy were more likely to be employed than natives with similar skills, but needed higher English to earn on par. — [MPI press release](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/press-release/does-immigrant-skills-gap-exist-us-report-finds-immigrants-score-below-us-born)
- **MPI (2024 data).** **47.1%** of foreign-born residents aged 5+ are LEP (speak English less than "very well"): 36.6% of naturalized citizens and 58.4% of noncitizens. LEP means self-reported "well", "not well" or "not at all" in the ACS. — [MPI State Language Data: US](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/data/state-profiles/state/language/US/US)
- **Total LEP population.** 26.5 million in 2022 (8.4% of the US population), up from 13.9 million in 1990. — [MPI USCCR testimony, March 2025 (PDF)](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/sites/default/files/publications/USCCR-Hofstetter-Testimony-March2025-FINAL.pdf)
- **Broader outcomes.** LEP immigrants have higher unemployment and poverty rates than English-proficient immigrants, and higher-skilled immigrants gain the most from English. — [Brookings "English Skills Embargo" (PDF)](https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/English-Skills-Embargo.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a university-educated Turk, the payoff from English is likely **larger** than average, because the Boston Fed finds the effect grows with education. Weak English is a main cause of "brain waste", where skilled immigrants work in jobs below their qualifications.
- English also gates practical life: licensing exams, CDL roadside checks, citizenship, and, after EO 14224, possibly fewer translated government services.

### Gaps
- I found no US data specific to Turkish-born immigrants' English proficiency or earnings in this session. The MPI or ACS country-of-birth tables would be the source.
- No current (2024-2026) national median-earnings comparison of LEP vs. English-proficient immigrants was found; the Brookings figures use 2012 data.

## 10. Free and low-cost English learning for immigrants in the US

### Takeaway
Federally funded adult-education English classes under **WIOA Title II** (AEFLA, including Integrated English Literacy & Civics) are generally **free** for adults aged 16+ who are English learners. In July 2025 the Education Department reclassified these programs as "federal public benefits" requiring immigration-status checks under PRWORA. That notice was **enjoined in plaintiff states on 10 Sep 2025** and **vacated by the D. Rhode Island court on 21 Sep 2026**. F-1 students in the US for full-time study are typically excluded from these classes.

### Cited Findings
- **Eligibility.** Adults aged 16+ who are not required to attend school and who lack a diploma, are basic-skills deficient, or are English language learners. — [Kansas WIOA Title II](https://workforce-ks.com/programs/workforceinnovationandopportunityact/WIOA-Title-II-Adult-Education-and-Literacy_151941.aspx); [Nevada WIOA Title II overview (PDF)](https://gowinn.nv.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Nancy-Olsen-WIOA-Title-II.pdf)
- **Free classes, with exclusions.** A West Virginia program offers free beginning, intermediate and advanced English classes. Federal rules bar serving people who came to the US "for the purpose of studying full-time in a university or a private language school". — [Monongalia County Technical Education Center](https://mtec.mono.k12.wv.us/page/general-info)
- **Integrated English Literacy and Civics Education (IELCE)** is a funded strand of the program. — [Illinois IELCE program](https://omb.illinois.gov/public/gata/csfa/Program.aspx?csfa=2879)

- **Litigation:**
  - **10 Sep 2025:** D.R.I. preliminary injunction blocked enforcement in the plaintiff states (NY, WA, RI and about 20 others plus DC). — [CSBA](https://blog.csba.org/legal-update-sept-2025/); [Feldesman](https://www.feldesman.com/court-grants-preliminary-injunction-staying-the-enforcement-of-new-prwora-interpretation-in-plaintiff-states/)
  - **21 Sep 2026:** Judge Mary S. McElroy granted summary judgment. The PRWORA Notices (DOJ, HHS, ED, DOL, HUD) "are declared unlawful under the APA and are ordered set aside and vacated". Defendants were permanently enjoined from enforcing them against plaintiffs. Agencies can still issue new rules after notice and comment.
  - DOL had already rescinded its own notice on 18 Mar 2026.

### Inferences
- A Turkish green-card holder, H-1B spouse or other lawful resident can usually join free adult-ESL classes. Classes run through school districts, community colleges and libraries in many areas.
- F-1 students cannot use these classes, so they must rely on their school's paid ESL or intensive English.
- Because the federal rule was vacated in court but could be re-issued through formal rulemaking, eligibility checks may differ by state, especially in states that were not plaintiffs.
