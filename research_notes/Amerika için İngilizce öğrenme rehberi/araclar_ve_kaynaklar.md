# Tools, Apps, Platforms and Resources for Learning (American) English, 2026: for a Turkish-Speaking Adult Moving to the USA

Research date: 2026-10-22. Unless noted, "TR App Store" prices are what the Turkish Apple App Store listing showed on 2026-10-22. The listings do not label billing periods, so where an item is called "monthly"/"annual" that is Apple's item name. USD prices come from the dated third-party sources cited. No verified USD/TRY exchange rate for October 2026 was collected, so TL figures are not converted.

General caveat for the report writer: many 2026 "pricing" pages are affiliate blogs or competitor blogs (SpeakShark sells a Speak competitor, Trancy sells a Language Reactor competitor, Clozemaster sells a Duolingo alternative, TalkDrill sells a Lingoda competitor). These are flagged where used. App-store prices vary by country, account and A/B test, so readers should always confirm the in-app price.

## 1. Spaced repetition / vocabulary (Anki + FSRS, pre-made decks, Quizlet, Memrise)

### Takeaway
Anki is still the strongest free option: desktop, AnkiDroid and AnkiWeb are free, and AnkiMobile (iOS) is a one-time purchase ($24.99 US, ₺1.299,99 in the TR App Store). FSRS has to be switched on in deck options; it is not the default. Quizlet Plus (about $36/yr) and Memrise Pro (about $60/yr, unconfirmed) are easier to use, but they are paid, and Memrise's price could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
**Anki**
- Desktop, AnkiDroid and AnkiWeb are free. The only paid piece is AnkiMobile on iOS, a one-time $24.99 purchase in the US store (checked 2026-09-08). Apple's data shows `hasInAppPurchases: false`. — [Mandarin Mosaic: Is Anki free (2026)](https://mandarinmosaic.com/blog/is-anki-free)
- Of 39 App Store storefronts checked, 33 charge more than the US price. EU storefronts charge €29.99. — [Mandarin Mosaic](https://mandarinmosaic.com/blog/is-anki-free); [AppFollow listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/ankimobile-flashcards/373493387?country=bb)
- TR App Store price of AnkiMobile: **₺1.299,99**, one-time, and the listing says "can be purchased once and then used on multiple devices in a household" (checked 2026-10-22). — [Apple App Store TR: AnkiMobile](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/ankimobile-flashcards/id373493387)
- Toolradar says "$24.99/month," but its own body text calls the purchase one-time, so the "/month" looks like an error. — [Toolradar Anki pricing](https://toolradar.com/tools/anki/pricing)
- **FSRS:** The official manual says "The Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS) is an alternative to Anki's legacy SuperMemo 2 (SM-2) algorithm" and tells users to "Enable FSRS under the 'FSRS' section, at the bottom of the deck options page." Desired retention defaults to 90%, which the manual calls "the most important setting." It advises staying below 97% because workload rises sharply near that level. Optimizing parameters "once every month is sufficient," and users should "not change the parameters manually or copy them from someone else." — [Anki Manual: Deck Options](https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html)
- CONFLICT: A third-party summary says Anki 23.10 (October 2023) made FSRS the *default*. The official manual describes FSRS as something you must enable, and the manual should be treated as authoritative. — [Mandarin Mosaic](https://mandarinmosaic.com/blog/is-anki-free) vs [Anki Manual](https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html)

**Word lists for building or choosing decks**
- The **New General Service List (NGSL)** has about 2,800 high-frequency words for L2 learners (Browne, Culligan, Phillips; first published 2013, updated 2016 and 2023) and gives "more than 92% coverage" of most general texts. — [Wikipedia: NGSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_General_Service_List)
- The NGSL project says to use only **newgeneralservicelist.com** for official downloads, because the older .org domain is no longer connected. Related lists on the site: NAWL (academic), TOEIC Service List, Business Service List, NGSL-Spoken. — [newgeneralservicelist.com](https://newgeneralservicelist.com/)

**Quizlet**
- Quizlet Plus costs $7.99/month or $35.99/year. Quizlet Plus Unlimited costs $9.99/month or $44.99/year (Mindomax checked the upgrade page on 2026-09-08). Paying monthly comes to $95.88/yr, against $35.99 annually. — [Mindomax: Quizlet Plus cost](https://www.mindomax.com/quizlet-plus-cost); [Nibble: Quizlet cost 2026](https://nibble-app.com/blog/quizlet-cost)

**Memrise**
- Pro is listed at about $8.99/month or $60/year (Subger, July 2026). The same page also says "plans starting from $14.99/month," which contradicts its own figure, so this is unconfirmed. — [Subger: Memrise](https://subger.com/en/service/memrise)

### Inferences
- For a Turkish adult planning a long stay in the US: use Anki (free on Android/desktop) with FSRS enabled at 85–90% retention. Build or choose decks from NGSL (A2–B2 core vocabulary), then NAWL if planning US college or academic work. An iPhone user pays ₺1.299,99 once, which over the years is cheaper than any subscription flashcard app.
- Quizlet is fine for quick lists, such as words from a tutor lesson, and the free tier may be enough. The paid tiers add little for a self-directed adult who already uses Anki.
- Suggested levels: Anki + NGSL at A2–B2, Anki + NAWL/sentence mining (for example via Language Reactor export) at B2–C1.

### Gaps
- The availability, quality and American-vs-British spelling of the pre-made decks named in the brief ("4000 Essential English Words," "Oxford 3000/5000," NGSL decks on AnkiWeb) could not be verified. Searches returned nothing on AnkiWeb shared decks. The writer should tell users to check AnkiWeb shared decks and whether audio is US-accented.
- No official Memrise price page was retrieved. Whether Memrise still offers a full English course for Turkish speakers was not checked.
- Quizlet's TL pricing was not collected.

## 2. Structured apps (Duolingo incl. Max/Video Call, Babbel, Busuu, Rosetta Stone, Mondly, Praktika, Speak, ELSA Speak, Cake, Lingoda): features, 2026 prices, worth it or not

### Takeaway
Prices are volatile and partly A/B-tested. Duolingo Max is reportedly being phased out, with Video Call moving into Super. Speaking-focused AI apps (Speak, Praktika, ELSA) cost roughly $80–$165/yr in the US and ₺700–₺8.000/yr in Türkiye. TR App Store prices are generally far lower than US prices, especially Busuu and Duolingo. Lingoda is the cheapest "live human class" option (about $6–8 per group class in Aug 2026).

### Inferences
- **Probably worth it:** (a) Busuu Premium bought in the TR store. It is very cheap in TL, has a free tier, and suits A1–B1 grammar and structure. (b) One AI speaking app (Praktika or Speak) for a few months at A2–B2 to beat speaking anxiety. Buy the monthly plan first instead of a trial that auto-converts to an annual plan. (c) Lingoda group classes at B1–C1 for structured live speaking on a budget.
- **Probably not worth it for this learner:** Rosetta Stone (expensive, immersion-only, with no explanations in Turkish). Mondly lifetime (cheap, but shallow past A2). Duolingo Max (reportedly being retired; Video Call is going into Super). Paying for Duolingo beyond A2–B1 in general.
- Duolingo free or Super (cheap in TL) is fine for habit-building at A1–A2. It should not be the main tool for someone who must function in the US.
- ELSA suits B1+ learners who specifically want an American accent. It is not a general course. Read the refund and trial terms.
- Note that Apple's TR prices apply to a Turkish Apple ID. After moving to the US with a US Apple ID, renewals will be at US prices.

### Gaps

- Rosetta Stone and Mondly TR prices were not collected. Whether Babbel offers English courses with a Turkish interface was not verified.
- No independent efficacy studies comparing these apps were collected.
- Lingoda's English teachers' accent mix (US vs UK) was not verified.

## 3. AI tools (ChatGPT voice, Claude, Gemini Live, dedicated AI tutors): speaking, writing correction, role-plays; limitations

### Takeaway
General AI assistants with voice are now the cheapest speaking-practice partners. Gemini Live is free. ChatGPT voice is free with a daily cap, and Plus gives about 5x the time. Claude voice mode is on all plans, but the free tier allows only about 20–30 voice messages. In Türkiye, ChatGPT Go costs ₺249,99 and Plus ₺999,99 in the App Store. Exact voice limits are not published.

### Cited Findings
- **ChatGPT:** Free users get Voice Mode with a daily allowance that OpenAI does not specify and that "can change with demand." Plus gets "more than 5x the free limit." Video and screen-share in voice chats are for paid users. Plus users get a warning when 15 minutes remain. — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/artificial-intelligence/now-that-chatgpt-voice-mode-is-free-is-it-even-worth-paying-for-chatgpt-plus-anymore); [TechCrunch (2024)](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/19/openai-brings-chatgpts-advanced-voice-mode-to-the-web)
- **ChatGPT TR App Store (2026-10-22):** ChatGPT Go ₺249,99. ChatGPT Plus ₺999,99 (also an item at ₺9.999,99, likely annual). Pro tiers ₺5.299,99 / ₺9.999,99 / ₺26.499,00. — [Apple App Store TR: ChatGPT](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/id6448311069)
- **Claude:** Voice mode is in beta on all plans, including Free, on mobile, desktop and web. Free users can expect "around 20 to 30 voice messages" before hitting the limit. It launched English-only, and the support page now mentions "many more languages." — [Claude Help: Using voice mode](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11101966-using-voice-mode); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/claudes-free-voice-mode-has-landed-heres-how-to-access-it)
- **Gemini Live:** Free for all users since September 2024. Expanded to 40+ languages. Can handle up to two languages on the same device. One source says it has no fixed free-tier cap. No 2026 confirmation of current limits was found. — [Revolgy](https://www.revolgy.com/insights/blog/google-expands-gemini-live-with-over-40-languages-for-ai-voice-chat); [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/gemini-live-languages-3487021/); [Ecosistema Startup (2026, ES)](https://ecosistemastartup.com/gemini-como-profesor-de-idiomas-gratis-ahorra-us168-al-ano/)
- **Dedicated AI tutors:** Duolingo Video Call (Lily), Speak and Praktika (prices in section 2). A competitor blog compares Speak with ChatGPT Voice for English practice. — [SpeakShark: Speak vs ChatGPT Voice](https://speakshark.com/blog/speak-vs-chatgpt-voice) (competitor-authored)

### Inferences
- **Practical method** (synthesis, not from a single source):
  1. Speaking: start a voice session with a role-play prompt. For example: "You are a receptionist at a US doctor's office. I am a new patient checking in. Speak American English at B1 level, keep turns short, and after every 5 turns give me 3 corrections and 2 more natural phrases." Useful scenarios for a newcomer: SSN/DMV office, opening a bank account, renting an apartment, pharmacy, job interview (behavioral STAR questions), calling customer service, small talk with neighbors or coworkers.
  2. Writing: paste a text (an email to a landlord, a cover letter) and ask for corrections in a table (original, corrected, short reason in Turkish), then rewrite it yourself.
  3. Ask for American-specific vocabulary (apartment/flat, check/bill, résumé, etc.).
- **Limitations:** voice models often accept non-native pronunciation without correcting it, so they are not a pronunciation teacher (ELSA or a human tutor is better for that). Corrections can be inconsistent. They sometimes "over-correct" into formal English. Usage caps apply. Speaking with them does not reproduce real-world speed, noise or accents. Privacy: avoid sharing personal documents. Treat as A2–C2. A1 learners may struggle without Turkish support, which the assistants can provide if asked.
- Free stack: Gemini Live + ChatGPT free voice. At ₺249,99/month, ChatGPT Go may be the cheapest paid upgrade, but whether Go includes more voice time was not verified.

### Gaps
- OpenAI's official Voice Mode FAQ returned HTTP 403, so the current free/Go/Plus voice limits and ChatGPT Go's voice features are unverified.
- No independent study of AI voice chat's effect on speaking proficiency was collected.
- Gemini Live's 2026 limits and Gemini paid-tier TL prices were not collected.

## 4. Human tutors (italki, Preply, Cambly, Lingoda, AmazingTalker, Verbling): 2025–2026 hourly prices; how to choose

### Takeaway
On marketplaces (italki, Preply, Verbling) tutors set their own rates. Community tutors charge roughly $5–20/hr. Professional or certified teachers charge roughly $15–60+/hr, and native US tutors sit at the higher end. Cambly is a flat subscription averaging about $20/hr. An experienced professional at $20–40/hr is considered a fair rate.

### Cited Findings
- **italki:** $4–$80/hour. Pay per lesson with credit in any amount. Trial lessons are paid and discounted. Preply: $2–$80+/hour, pay-per-lesson or a subscription of 4+ lessons per 28 days. Trials are paid but refundable, with a free tutor swap. Over 40,000 English tutors. Cambly: about $20/hour on average, a subscription with weekly booked time, the same price for every tutor, no trial. Verbling: $5–$100/hour, one free 30-min trial, strictest vetting. The author considers "$20 to $40/hour a fair rate for an experienced professional" and warns that some italki/Preply tutors claimed native or C2 status without evidence. (Published 25 Sep 2026, updated 29 Sep 2026.) — [Leonardo English: italki vs Preply vs Cambly vs Verbling (2026)](https://www.leonardoenglish.com/blog/italki-vs-preply-vs-cambly-vs-verbling-review)
- italki community tutors cost $5–15/hr and professional teachers $15–40+/hr per one source. Another gives $8–20 and $20–60+. Preply runs $5–10 for entry-level tutors and $30–50 for premium native-speaker tutors. — [learnenglish.life: Cambly vs italki vs Preply](https://learnenglish.life/compare/cambly-vs-italki-vs-preply/); [EngVarta comparison (competitor)](https://engvarta.com/engvarta-vs-cambly-vs-preply-vs-italki-comparison/)
- italki definitions: "Professional teachers have training as educators and/or extensive professional teaching experience. Community tutors... provide students with conversation practice." Professional teachers must hold a teaching certificate. — [italki support](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002978766); [FluentU italki review](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/italki/)
- italki's commission reportedly rose from 15% to 21% for single lessons, with a sliding scale on packages (2025 guide). This affects tutors' rates indirectly. — [Andromeda Jones Substack (2025)](https://andromedajones.substack.com/p/online-language-tutoring-a-viable)
- Cambly "starts at $18.75/month" according to an AI-generated comparison page, unverified. — [Pulse Signal](https://getpulsesignal.com/compare/cambly-vs-smartickmethod)
- AmazingTalker: from about $10 per 50-minute class, with a free rebook if unhappy (third-party, unverified). — [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/?p=25731)
- Lingoda private 1-on-1 costs about $43/class (see section 2). — [OnlineCourseing](https://onlinecourseing.com/?p=2050556)

### Inferences
- **How to choose (synthesis):** (1) Filter by country = United States and listen to the intro video for accent. (2) Prefer "professional teacher" for grammar or exam goals, and a "community tutor" for cheap conversation volume. (3) Book 2–3 trial lessons and compare. (4) Check reviews for preparation, error correction and homework, and ask for written corrections after each lesson. (5) Give the tutor concrete US-life scenarios (job interview, doctor, landlord). (6) Avoid long prepaid packages until a tutor is proven.
- Budget model: 1–2 community-tutor conversation sessions per week (about $10–15 each) plus AI practice daily. Add a professional US teacher ($25–40) monthly or for interview preparation.
- Levels: community tutors suit A2–C1 conversation. Professional teachers suit any level, and A1 learners may benefit from a Turkish-speaking teacher.

### Gaps
- Official current Cambly pricing (the subscribe page returned 404) and Preply's US-tutor average price (the page returned 404) were not retrieved.
- No TL pricing for tutor platforms was collected. Turkish-speaking English tutors' typical rates were not checked.
- Whether Cambly or other platforms let you filter specifically for US accents was not verified.

## 5. Language exchange and Turkish-specific in-person options (Tandem, HelloTalk, clubs, American Spaces/Corners, EducationUSA, Fulbright, TAD)

### Takeaway
HelloTalk and Tandem are free, with optional VIP/Pro at about $5–14/month. In Türkiye, US-government-linked options include the American Language Corner at Atatürk University (Erzurum), EducationUSA advising centers (Ankara at the Turkish American Association, Istanbul, a virtual İzmir office) and the Turkish American Association (TAD), which has emphasized American English since 1951. No official list of American Spaces in Türkiye could be retrieved because the embassy site blocked access.

### Cited Findings
- HelloTalk VIP is about $5–10/month according to HelloTalk's own 2026 blog, or $6.99/month according to Toolradar. Tandem Pro costs $13.99/month or $79.99/year. — [HelloTalk blog 2026](https://www.hellotalk.com/blog/language-app-subscription-worth-it-2026); [Toolradar: Tandem pricing](https://toolradar.com/tools/tandem/pricing)
- **American Spaces network:** "over 620"/"over 700" locations in 140+ countries, managed by US embassies. American Corners are partner sites, often in libraries or universities. Typical offerings are American English courses, conversation clubs and internet access. ## 9. Writing (Grammarly, LanguageTool, AI feedback, Write & Improve)

### Takeaway
Write & Improve (Cambridge) is free, unlimited, CEFR-scored (A1–C2) and has 250+ tasks, so it is the best free structured writing feedback. ## 12. Free resources in the USA after arrival (library ESL, community college/adult ed, USA Learns, literacy volunteers, Mango)

### Takeaway
Free options are plentiful: USA Learns (free, run by the Sacramento County Office of Education, with Citizenship and "Access America" courses), Mango Languages and Transparent Language free with a public library card, publicly funded adult-education ESOL (free or nearly free, often in the evenings), noncredit community college ESL (often tuition-free), and ProLiteracy's 1,200 member programs in all 50 states. Eligibility can depend on immigration status.

### Cited Findings
- **USA Learns** (usalearns.org): free and run by the Sacramento County Office of Education. Courses: 1st English Course, English 1 Plus, 2nd English Course, Practice English & Reading (speaking, listening, vocabulary, pronunciation, reading, writing, grammar), *Access America* (life in the US), *USA Learns Citizenship* (naturalization interview prep) and *Skills for the Nursing Assistant*. Works on phones, tablets and computers. Site in English and Spanish. — [USA Learns](https://www.usalearns.org/)
- **Mango Languages:** free for library cardholders; sign up with your card number; mobile apps available. ESL/ELL course counts vary by library (e.g., 16–17 at some, 70+ listed by LAPL). Transparent Language Online is also free with a library card at some libraries. — [Sonoma County Library](https://sonomalibrary.org/node/344); [LAPL](https://lapl.org/node/111674); [EveryLibrary](https://action.everylibrary.org/learn_a_new_language_for_free_using_your_library_card)
- **Adult education ESOL:** publicly funded classes are "generally free or at very little cost," often in the evenings. For example, San Jacinto College's classes in Texas are free through grant programs. — [NLD: Adult education for immigrants](https://www.nld.org/adult-education-for-immigrants-and-refugees); [NLD: San Jacinto](https://www.nld.org/san-jacinto-adult-education-program)
- **Community colleges:** noncredit ESL can be free. Citrus College (CA) students "do not pay to attend," but buy a textbook and parking. Credit ESL costs money and can use up financial aid. — [Citrus College ESL](https://www.citruscollege.edu/ce/Pages/ESL.aspx); [CCRC (2026)](https://ccrc.tc.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/community-colleges-providers-adult-education.pdf)

- **ProLiteracy:** 1,200 member programs in all 50 states plus DC. Local volunteer programs offer free English tutoring (e.g., Franklin, MA public library). — [Franklin MA](https://www.franklinma.gov/node/410636); [Bergenfield Library](https://www.bergenfieldlibrary.org/become-a-tutor/)
